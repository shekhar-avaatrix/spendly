Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account details: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense records: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the service.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the application.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the application; these providers access your data only as necessary to deliver the service and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We do not integrate with any advertising networks or analytics platforms that track you across other websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your data, please reach out through the contact information provided in the application. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
