Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or government-issued identifiers.

We may also collect basic technical data such as:

  • Your browser type and version
  • Pages visited within the Service and timestamps
  • Your device's operating system

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide and maintain your account and expense records
  • Display spending summaries, trends, and category breakdowns to you
  • Respond to support requests you submit
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the Service

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon verified request, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to serve typefaces. Requests to load fonts are made directly to Google's servers and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No expense data is transmitted to Google or any other third party.

We do not integrate with analytics providers, advertising networks, or social media platforms. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and users will be notified.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
